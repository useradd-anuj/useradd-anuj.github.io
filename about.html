About

👋 Hello, I'm Anuj Patel, an aspiring DevOps and SecOps engineer with a passion for building efficient, secure, and scalable solutions. I’m currently pursuing a Computer Systems Technology Diploma at Saskatchewan Polytechnic, with an expected graduation in June 2025. My studies, combined with hands-on projects, have provided me with a strong foundation in software development, system administration, networking, cybersecurity, and cloud computing.

I’m particularly interested in integrating cloud services, optimizing development workflows, and ensuring secure IT environments. My technical expertise includes working with AWS, Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines, among others. Through personal projects and coursework, I’ve had the opportunity to work on microservices architecture, RESTful APIs, and automation tools like Ansible and Jenkins. These experiences have helped me build integrations that bridge systems and streamline data flow.

In my spare time, I continue to learn and grow through personal projects, many of which you can find on my GitHub. I also share my knowledge and insights on industry trends and best practices on Medium.

I’m always looking for new opportunities to collaborate, innovate, and contribute to impactful projects. Feel free to reach out to me at patel-anuj@outlook.com – let’s connect!
